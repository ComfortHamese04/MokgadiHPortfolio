Networking is a key part of my practical experience. I investigate connectivity problems, identify devices on networks and help restore access to network resources.

  • Using SSH to connect to Linux machines, check access and perform remote troubleshooting.
  • Investigating communication issues between workstations, servers and other network-connected equipment.
  • Supporting remote access using SSH and VNC, including Raspberry Pi devices.
  • Developing a practical understanding of how hardware, operating systems and networks interact.
02 · Hardware, printers & peripherals

I support the physical equipment that students and staff rely on, from workstations and displays to printers and connected peripherals.

  • Troubleshooting desktop PCs, monitors, keyboards, mice and peripheral connections.

  • Supporting troubleshooting and configuration in virtualised environments.

I enjoy getting hands-on with operating systems and devices, particularly when diagnosing a problem requires understanding what happens behind the interface.
